Sohrai: privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Sohrai is a free game made by Dulor (Tumdah). It has no accounts, no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress, settings, language, chosen animal and a random player id made by the game. Clearing the app's data removes them.

What the game's server keeps

  • Leaderboard: if you play a scored round, the name you typed (you may leave it empty or invent one), your score, your level and your random player id.
  • Village: if you choose a village and district, that choice, so your score counts for your village.
  • Keep my progress: if you use it, a copy of your progress stored under a code that only you are shown.
  • Evening reminders: if you turn them on, the push address your browser gives us, so one reminder a day can be sent. Turn them off in the game or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Step counts: anonymous totals such as "rounds played today". They carry no id and cannot be linked to you.

Your IP address is used for a moment to limit abuse. It is not stored with your player id.

Village talk

  • The village talk is a public chat. If you say something there, every player can read it, together with the name you typed and your village if you chose one. Do not put anything private in it.
  • Messages are deleted after 3 days. Links, phone numbers and abuse are refused. You can report any message or hide a person; reported messages are hidden.
  • While the game is open it tells the server every half minute that you are there, so players can see how many are in the village. This uses your random player id, not your name.

Playing with friends

  • In a room, the other players see your name, your character and your score.
  • Voice chat is off until you tap the microphone. Your voice goes to the players in your room (directly, or through a Cloudflare relay). It is never recorded or stored.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or collect your contacts, location, photos or phone number.

Deleting your data

Leaderboard scores are daily. To have your name, saved progress, village choice or anything you said in the village talk removed, write to the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page and include your progress code or the name you used.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
