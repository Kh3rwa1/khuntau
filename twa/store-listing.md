```
Sohrai is the harvest festival of the Santal people. This game lets you live it: wake in a mud house, paint the wall, oil your animal's horns, beat the tamak at the akhra, and on Khuntau day step into the ring with a tied bull and snatch the pitha from the post before he gets you.

LIVE THE FIVE DAYS
• Um Hilok: wake up, drink handi, paint the wall, choose your animal and oil its horns
• Dakay Din: draw the alpana before your door
• Khuntau: garland the animal, drum at the akhra, face the bull
• Jale: the Sarpa enej goes from house to house
• Hako Katkom: catch fish at the pond
Each day opens on the next real day. Nothing is locked, but do the wrong thing on the wrong day and the Majhi will tell you off.

THE RING
• Hide to make the bull charge, roll aside, grab the pitha while he is stunned
• Five stages: he learns to feint, to charge twice and to bend his run toward you
• Three lamps are your lives. A handi pot gives one back
• Golden pitha, combos, slow motion and a crowd that flinches with you

A VILLAGE THAT IS ALIVE
• Drummers and dancers, a pork stall, a woman carrying water, chickens, goats, ducks and sleeping dogs
• Archery at a banana-trunk target, fishing with a bamboo rod, a tamak and tumdak rhythm game
• Paddy fields in evening light all round the village

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
• Open a room, walk the village together and start a round at the khunt
• Voice chat if you want it
• Daily leaderboard, and village against village: play for your own village and district

MADE FOR PHONES
• Runs on budget Android phones and adjusts its own quality
• Works offline after the first load
• English and Hindi
• Free. No ads, no accounts, no purchases

Made by Dulor (Tumdah), with music by Dulor. Johar!
```

## App content (Policy > App content)

- **Ads:** No ads.
- **App access:** All functionality is available without special access (no login).
- **Target audience:** 13 and over. Do not tick the under-13 groups: the game has voice chat between players and shows drinking handi, and the Families rules would apply.
- **Content rating questionnaire** (category: Game). Answer truthfully; these are the ones that matter:
  - Violence: yes, mild and cartoon-like (a bull knocks the player over; no blood). The pork stall shows cut meat and a killed pig.
  - Alcohol: yes, the player drinks handi (rice beer) as part of the festival.
  - Users can interact or exchange content: yes (the village talk: a public text chat between all players; voice chat in rooms; names on the leaderboard; village names). The village talk has automatic filters (links, phone numbers, handles, abuse words), in-app Report on every message (two reports hide it) and Hide this person; voice is not moderated. Google's user-generated-content policy asks for exactly these (report, block, a way to remove content: `MUTED_TAGS` / `CHAT_ON` in `src/worker.js`).
  - Shares location: no. Purchases: no. Gambling, sexual content, drugs, bad language: no.
  - Expect a rating around 12+/Teen, not "Everyone".
- **Data safety:** import `twa/data-safety.csv` (Data safety > Import from CSV), then check every screen before saving. It holds these answers (Claude's reading of the form against what `src/worker.js` stores; Dulor signs it):
  - Collects or shares user data: Yes. Encrypted in transit: Yes. Users can ask for deletion: Yes (by email; see the privacy page).
  - Personal info > Name: collected, optional, app functionality (leaderboard name; a nickname counts).
  - Device or other IDs: collected, required, app functionality (a random player id made by the game).
  - App activity > App interactions: collected, app functionality and analytics (scores, progress backup, anonymous step counts).
  - Audio > Voice or sound recordings: collected, optional, processed ephemerally, app functionality (voice chat; never stored).
  - Messages > Other in-app messages: collected, optional, app functionality (the village talk: what you type is public to all players and deleted after 3 days).
  - Nothing is shared with third parties. Location: not collected (village and district are typed in, not read from the phone).
- **Government apps, financial features, health, news:** No to all.
